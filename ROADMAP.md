# Spatial Humanities 2026 roadmap

This repository brings together the public research, teaching and demonstration
materials for Spatial Humanities 2026. It uses the separately maintained
[`spatio-textual`](https://github.com/IgnatiusEzeani/spatio-textual) package for
the underlying NLP and spatial-analysis capabilities.

## Available now

- A full-day workshop organised as ten Google Colab notebooks, progressing from
  manual annotation and rules to contextual NLP, LLM-assisted extraction,
  adjudication, mapping and responsible Spatial AI.
- A Streamlit demonstration for analysing narrative text, comparing methods,
  inspecting evidence and exploring spatial outputs.
- Public-safe teaching examples and lightweight fallback routes that do not
  require a GPU or paid API key.
- Reproducible benchmarks, evaluation protocols and documented results for
  named-entity recognition, journey extraction and affect analysis.
- Shared schemas for annotations, model outputs, provenance and human review.

## Planned conference improvements

  and responsible use of external AI services.

## Methodological direction

The project does not treat manual annotation, rules, statistical NLP,
transformers and LLMs as a simple progression from older to better methods. It
compares their strengths, limitations and resource requirements while keeping
machine-generated interpretations traceable to the source text and open to
human review.

## Data and access

Only distributable or explicitly public-safe text is included in the workshop
and demonstration. Controlled archival material is represented through
permitted examples, schemas or aggregate research results rather than source
transcripts.
